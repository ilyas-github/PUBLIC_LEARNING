Search coverage

Search checks root letters, English-friendly root spellings (for example, alh, amn, aty), letter names (such as alif lam ha), transliteration, English and Urdu meanings, recorded derived words, their glosses, and example-ayah text and translations. Arabic diacritics and hamza variations are normalized for easier matching.

Select a root from the library or search in English, Urdu, Arabic or transliteration.
Source and scope

Root and lemma data, parts of speech and occurrence counts: supplied Quranic Arabic Corpus v0.4 dataset. Uthmani Arabic text and Fateh Muhammad Jalandhari Urdu translation: as attributed in the supplied dataset to Tanzil. English and Urdu glosses are curated study aids in the supplied file, not independent tafsir. The source intentionally lists up to four most frequent distinct derivatives per root; it is not an exhaustive list of every Qur'anic form. Five selected roots now have structured reviewed morphology/context entries (36 derivative records). The remaining roots retain the base corpus inventory and have not yet undergone the same expanded editorial review. Do not infer unattested conjugations.

For local use, keep index.html and Dataset.js in the same folder. Dataset.js is deliberately separate so it can be maintained without editing the interface.
