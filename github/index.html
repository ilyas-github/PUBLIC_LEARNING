Quranic Arabic Roots — Top 100

Learn the 100 most frequent roots of the Holy Quran, with words taken only from the Quran itself.

Every word is cited to its verse and cross-verified between the Quranic Arabic Corpus and the Tanzil text.

Sources & verification. Quran text: Tanzil (Uthmani). Roots, lemmas and word analysis: Quranic Arabic Corpus v0.4 (University of Leeds). Verse Urdu translation: Fateh Muhammad Jalandhari (via Tanzil). English/Urdu word meanings were curated for this tool and cross-checked against published word-by-word references (Shaikh & Khatri, English; Dr. Farhat Hashmi, Urdu). In an automated cross-check of all 77,429 words of the Quran, 77,423 (99.99%) matched exactly between the Corpus analysis and the Tanzil text; all 358 example words in this app are among the verified matches.
