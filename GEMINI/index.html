ⓘ Methodology & Verification

This offline prototype is designed for rigorous educational exploration of Qur'anic roots.

Three-Level Scrutiny Protocol:

  • 1. Occurrence Verification: Verifies exact Qur'anic spelling, Surah, and Ayah presence.
  • 2. Morphological Verification: Ensures the derived word is genuinely attributable to the root without duplication.
  • 3. Contextual Translation: Separates core root concept from context-specific translation in English and Urdu.

Data Scaling Strategy:

Roots are ingested in verified 50-root batches. Interface logic is separated completely from data (rootsData.js) to allow seamless offline expansion up to 1,800 roots.
